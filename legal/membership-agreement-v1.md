## 1. Purpose

The Member has been admitted to the **{{seat_label}}** seat of the Verana
Council — the sole body that governs and secures the live Verana network — and
by this Agreement accepts the binding terms of Council membership.

## 2. Membership status

2.1 **One status.** Upon execution and the Verein's incorporation, the Member is
a statutory member of the Verein, a General Assembly voter (one member, one
vote), and a validator operator.

2.2 **Free membership.** No dues and no capital contribution are payable. The
statutes of the Verein exclude personal financial liability of members
(Art. 75a ZGB).

2.3 Pre-incorporation seatings are ratified en bloc at the constitutive General
Assembly of the Verein.

## 3. Validator obligation

3.1 Operating a validator node on the Verana mainnet is a **mandatory and
inseparable** component of Council membership from mainnet launch; voting rights
are exercised exclusively via an active validator node.

3.2 During the formation period the Member **should** operate a validator node
on the Verana testnet — the readiness step for the genesis validator set.

3.3 Validator running costs are borne by the Member and offset, after launch, by
on-chain validator rewards. Validator terms are fixed, with formal renewal.

## 4. Governance & conduct

4.1 The Member adheres to the Network Governance Framework, the ECS-EGF, the
Verein's statutes, and the Council Code of Conduct (published in
`verana-labs/verana-council-gov`).

4.2 Pre-mainnet, admissions and governance decisions use the Council's
provisional mechanism; at mainnet launch all Council voting moves on-chain.
