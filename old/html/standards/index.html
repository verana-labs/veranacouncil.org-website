Composition, not replacement

The Council composes standards. It does not replace them.

The Verana network is built on open standards authored by chartered standards bodies. The Council's role is to compose those primitives into two CC-BY-SA 4.0 composition specs (the Verifiable Trust spec and the VPR spec) and to govern the infrastructure those specs operate on. The Council intends to upstream both composition specs to a chartered standards body (W3C or DIF, by Council decision) once the network is operational.

The Council's standards coordination role

The Standards Liaison Committee maintains working relationships with each of the above bodies. Its scope:

  • Track normative changes to upstream specifications and assess impact on the Verifiable Trust spec and VPR spec.
  • Represent Verana's composition work in upstream working groups.
  • Coordinate the eventual upstream of the Verifiable Trust and VPR composition specs to a chartered body.
