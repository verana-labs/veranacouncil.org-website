## **RECITALS**

**WHEREAS**, the Verana Foundation (in Formation) is engaged in the organization, development, and prospective launch of the **Verana Network**, a decentralized trust network designed to support the operation of verifiable credentials, decentralized identifiers, trust registries, verifiable services, and associated governance frameworks;

**WHEREAS**, the Verana Network is intended to be governed, upon launch, by a governance body known as the **Verana Council**, which is expected to consist of reputable, independent institutions that will collectively (i) participate in on-chain governance; (ii) operate validator nodes essential to the integrity, neutrality, and transparency of the network; and (iii) uphold the foundational principles embodied in the Verana Governance Charter and related governance instruments, including one-member-one-vote neutrality, diversity of representation, independence, and technical robustness;

**WHEREAS**, the Prospective Initial Council Member desires to express its good-faith intent to participate as an **Initial Council Member** of the Verana Council at or prior to the launch of the mainnet of the Verana Network, subject to its satisfaction of the requirements set forth herein and in any subsequently executed binding agreements;

**WHEREAS**, the Verana Foundation (in Formation), through 2060 OÜ acting as its designated organizer, desires to record the shared intentions of the Parties regarding pre-launch collaboration, preparatory governance activities, technical readiness, and the procedural steps required for the Prospective Initial Council Member to be formally appointed as an Initial Council Member upon mainnet launch;

**WHEREAS**, the Parties acknowledge and agree that this MoU is not intended to constitute and shall not constitute a binding appointment of the Prospective Initial Council Member to the Verana Council, nor shall it create or confer any governance rights, fiduciary obligations, voting authority, or legal membership in the Foundation or Council, all of which shall arise only upon the execution of additional binding agreements and satisfaction of expressly defined conditions; and

**WHEREAS**, the Parties wish to set forth their mutual understanding, without binding effect (except where expressly stated herein), regarding the preparatory commitments, representations, and cooperation necessary for the Prospective Initial Council Member to become eligible for eventual appointment as an Initial Council Member of the Verana Council.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

# **1\. DEFINITIONS**

1.1 **“Associate Membership”** means the preliminary class of membership in the Foundation required of any entity that intends to serve as a Council Member, including the Prospective Initial Council Member, and which must be obtained through an application, due diligence review, and formal approval by the Foundation (or its organizers prior to incorporation).

1.2 **“Council Membership Agreement”** means the binding agreement that must be executed by each entity in order to become a formal member of the Verana Council, as may be drafted, amended, or adopted by the Foundation and/or the Council.

1.3 **“Genesis File”** means the canonical configuration file or set of files that define the initial state of the Verana Network at mainnet launch, including validator set composition, trust deposit allocations, network parameters, and other foundational settings.

1.4 **“Initial Trust Deposit”** means the trust deposit required for validator activation, which for Initial Council Members shall be allocated (“voided”) from the initial token supply and recorded in the Genesis File.

1.5 **“Initial Council Member”** means a Council Member designated to participate in the inaugural validator set and governance cohort at the launch of the Verana Network’s mainnet.

1.6 **“Validator Node”** or **“Validator”** means a network node operated by a Council Member and used to perform ledger block validation, consensus participation, and on-chain governance functions in accordance with the technical specifications of the Verana Network.

# **2\. NATURE AND EFFECT OF THIS MOU**

2.1 This MoU is intended solely as a statement of the Parties’ mutual intentions and shall not, except as expressly provided herein, create any legally binding obligations upon either Party. For the avoidance of doubt, this MoU does not constitute:

(a) admission of the Prospective Initial Council Member into the Foundation;

(b) appointment to the Verana Council;

(c) conferral of any governance, voting, or fiduciary rights;

(d) authorization to act on behalf of the Foundation or the Verana Network; or

(e) a partnership, joint venture, agency, association, or any form of legally recognized collaboration.

2.2 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Sections **7 (Confidentiality)**, **10 (Governing Law)**, and **11 (Dispute Resolution)** shall be legally binding upon the Parties.

2.3 The Parties acknowledge that the Foundation, upon its incorporation, may elect, but is not obligated, to ratify this MoU. Until such ratification, 2060 OÜ shall act solely in its capacity as promoter of the Foundation.

# **3\. INTENTION TO SERVE AS INITIAL COUNCIL MEMBER**

3.1 The Prospective Initial Council Member hereby expresses its non-binding intention and good-faith commitment to seek formal appointment as an Initial Council Member and to perform the obligations associated therewith, including but not limited to operating a Validator Node, participating in governance, and supporting the decentralized, neutral, and transparent operation of the Verana Network.

3.2 The Foundation expresses its non-binding intention, subject to satisfaction of the requirements herein and all applicable governance rules, to consider and provisionally include the Prospective Initial Council Member as an Initial Council Member in the Genesis File.

# **4\. PREREQUISITE: ASSOCIATE MEMBERSHIP IN THE FOUNDATION**

4.1 The Prospective Initial Council Member acknowledges that formal appointment as a Council Member is expressly conditioned upon its acceptance as an **Associate Member** of the Foundation.

4.2 The Prospective Initial Council Member agrees to submit a complete and accurate application for Associate Membership and to cooperate fully with all due diligence, verification, compliance, and onboarding processes deemed necessary by the Foundation.

4.3 Appointment to the Council shall not be effective unless and until Associate Membership is granted. For the avoidance of doubt, acceptance as an Associate Member remains subject to the sole discretion of the Foundation.

# **5\. MANDATORY OPERATION OF VALIDATOR NODE**

5.1 The Parties agree and acknowledge that operating a Validator Node is a mandatory and inseparable component of serving as a Council Member.

5.2 The Prospective Initial Council Member shall, at its sole cost and responsibility, ensure the timely setup, testing, configuration, security hardening, and readiness of its Validator Node for inclusion in the genesis validator set.

5.3 Voting rights on the Verana Network are exercised exclusively via an active Validator Node. Accordingly, failure to maintain a functioning Validator Node shall result in suspension or loss of governance authority, notwithstanding any executed Council Membership Agreement.

# **6\. TRUST DEPOSIT ALLOCATION AND REQUIREMENTS**

6.1 The Prospective Initial Council Member acknowledges that the Trust Deposit required for validator activation shall be allocated from the initial token supply and recorded in the Genesis File.

6.2 The exact amount of the Initial Trust Deposit shall be as stated in the Genesis File. For the avoidance of doubt, such amount shall not be specified in this MoU and may be amended prior to launch.

6.3 The allocated Trust Deposit is subject to the same slashing, governance, and accountability mechanisms as Trust Deposits provided by future Council Members.

6.4 Should the Prospective Initial Council Member fail to activate its Validator Node by the deadlines established by the Foundation or Council, the Foundation may, to the fullest extent permissible under applicable law:

(a) revoke the Prospective Initial Council Member’s provisional designation;

(b) freeze, reassign, or otherwise disposition the allocated Trust Deposit; and

(c) designate a replacement Initial Council Member.

# **7\. CONFIDENTIALITY**

7.1 Each Party shall keep strictly confidential all non-public information obtained from the other in connection with this MoU, including governance drafts, technical materials, Validator configuration specifications, risk assessments, Genesis File previews, private keys (which shall never be shared), operational documents, and internal communications.

7.2 Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this MoU.

# **8\. FUTURE COUNCIL MEMBER ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS**

8.1 After mainnet launch, admission of any new Council Member shall require approval by a **two-thirds (⅔) supermajority** of the then-seated Council.

8.2 No future Council Member may be appointed unless it has:

(a) obtained Associate Membership;

(b) paid the applicable Trust Deposit; and

(c) activated its Validator Node.

# **9\. TERM AND TERMINATION**

9.1 This MoU shall remain in effect until terminated by either Party upon written notice, or automatically upon execution of a Council Membership Agreement by the Prospective Initial Council Member.

9.2 Termination of this MoU shall not affect the enforceability of Sections expressly surviving termination, including Sections 7, 10, and 11\.

# **10\. GOVERNING LAW**

10.1 This MoU and any dispute arising out of or relating to this MoU shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **Estonia**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

# **11\. DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

11.1 Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or in connection with this MoU shall be resolved exclusively through confidential arbitration administered by the Arbitration Court of the Estonian Chamber of Commerce and Industry in accordance with its rules then in effect.

11.2 The seat of arbitration shall be Tallinn, Estonia. The language of arbitration shall be English.

# **12\. NO WAIVER; NO ASSIGNMENT**

12.1 No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right shall be deemed a waiver.

12.2 The Prospective Initial Council Member may not assign this MoU without prior written consent of the Foundation.

# **13\. RATIFICATION UPON INCORPORATION**

13.1 Upon incorporation of the Verana Foundation, the Foundation may elect to ratify and adopt this MoU. Upon such ratification, all references herein to the Foundation shall apply to the incorporated entity, and 2060 OÜ shall cease acting as its representative for these purposes.

# **14\. MISCELLANEOUS**

14.1 If any provision of this MoU is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

14.2 This MoU constitutes the entire understanding of the Parties regarding its subject matter.
