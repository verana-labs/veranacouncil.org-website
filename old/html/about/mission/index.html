About the Council

Governance for the open public trust layer.

We are a non-profit association stewarding the two frameworks that let any service, organization, person, or AI agent prove who they are on a public, permissionless, open-standard trust registry, and securing the chain on which those frameworks run.

Mission

To author, operate, and evolve (transparently and under one-member-one-vote rules) the two governance frameworks that make the Verana network a neutral public trust infrastructure: the Network Governance Framework (the rules every ecosystem must respect) and the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework (the rules for the four Essential Credential Schemas).

What we do

  1. Author the Network Governance Framework. The constitutional layer: the requirements every EGF on Verana must satisfy, the graduated risk-management rubric, the dispute-resolution process, and the chain-evolution process.
  2. Author the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework. The rules under which the Council selects Issuer Grantors and Issuers for each of the four Essential Credential Schemas in each jurisdiction.
  3. Publish the Template EGF. A working scaffold for ecosystems building their own sector EGF. Not an approval gate.
  4. Operate the chain. Every Council Member runs a validator node, directly or via a qualified delegated operator under the Member's accountability. The validator set is restricted to Council Members.
  5. Enforce the Code of Conduct. Including the conflict-of-interest cooldown (members recuse on decisions affecting direct competitors) and the seat-diversity rule (one voice per sector × region).
  6. Coordinate with standards bodies. W3C, DIF, Hyperledger, OpenWallet Foundation, OpenID Foundation, Trust over IP. The Council intends to upstream the Verifiable Trust and VPR composition specs to a chartered standards body once the network is operational.

What we are not

  • Not a chartered standards body. Verana publishes two CC-BY-SA 4.0 composition specs that the Council intends to upstream. The Council governs the infrastructure and the ECS-EGF; it does not author standards.
  • Not a product vendor. Wallets, agents, IDV services, reputation services run on Verana; none of them are built or operated by the Council.
  • Not a sector EGF authority. Any ecosystem can author and operate an EGF on Verana provided it respects the Network GF. The Council publishes a Template EGF as a working scaffold, not as an approval gate.
  • Not a grant-making body. Grants, developer programs, and treasury operations are the remit of the Verana Foundation (Cayman, separate entity). Cross-reference only.
  • Not single-company controlled. One-member-one-vote, seat-diversity enforced, Code of Conduct binding.
