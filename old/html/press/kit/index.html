Boilerplate

One-paragraph

The Verana Council Association is a non-profit Swiss Verein that governs the Verana public trust network. The Council authors and operates the Verana Network Governance Framework (the rules every ecosystem on the network must respect) and the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework (the rules for the four Essential Credential Schemas), and secures the chain on which those frameworks run. One-member-one-vote. Seat diversity enforced by (sector × region) rule. Incorporates Q3 2026.

Three-sentence

The Verana Council Association is the governance body of the open public trust layer. A Swiss Verein incorporating in Q3 2026, the Council authors the Network Governance Framework and the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework under one-member-one-vote rules. Every Member operates a validator node on the Verana chain, making the Council the body that secures and governs the infrastructure it stewards.
