Constitutional document

The Association's operating statutes.

The Bylaws are the constitutional document of the Verana Council Association Swiss Verein. They define the Association's purpose, its organs, voting thresholds, membership classes, committee structures, and amendment procedures.

Draft outline

  1. Preamble. The Association's purpose.
  2. Name, seat, and duration. Swiss Verein, seat in [CANTON], unlimited duration.
  3. Object. The Council's mission (see Mission).
  4. Membership. Classes (Founding Member, Member, Public-Sector Observer), admission process, rights, obligations, termination.
  5. Organs. General Assembly, Board, Committees, Founding Steward (sunset at incorporation).
  6. General Assembly. Composition, convening, voting thresholds, quorum, written-consent procedures.
  7. Board. Composition, term limits (two-year staggered), election, removal, powers.
  8. Committees. Standing committees listed in Structure; charters adopted by GA.
  9. Validator operation. Member obligation; qualified-delegated-operator provision.
  10. Finances. Non-profit status, funding sources, external audit obligations.
  11. Code of Conduct. Binding on all participants; see Code of Conduct.
  12. Dispute Resolution. Internal procedure; venue and governing law for external appeals.
  13. Amendment. Thresholds for Bylaw amendments, framework amendments, and Membership structure changes.
  14. Dissolution. Procedure and residual-asset distribution.

How Members review the draft

Founding Members' General Counsel teams receive the current draft under NDA. Written comments are aggregated by the Nominations & Conduct Committee and incorporated into the pre-incorporation draft. Final text is ratified by the Incorporation General Assembly.
