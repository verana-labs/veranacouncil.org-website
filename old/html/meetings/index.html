General Assembly

Quarterly meetings. Public record.

The Council's General Assembly meets quarterly. Agendas are published 14 days in advance; minutes and voting records are published within 30 days. Open-session segments are public and may include Observers and press.

Meeting structure

Each General Assembly has:

  • Open session: mission-level updates, framework amendment proposals, general business. Public.
  • Member session: committee reports, votes on Member-only matters, sanctions decisions. Members and Observers only; minutes redacted only where legally required.
  • Closed session: personnel matters, legal privilege, confidential sanctions details. Minutes summarized publicly; underlying deliberations not disclosed.

What gets voted

  • Framework amendments (Network GF, ECS-EGF, Template EGF).
  • ECS Issuer Grantor and Issuer selections.
  • Sanctions at the Suspension and Slashing rubric steps.
  • Bylaw amendments and seat-structure changes.
  • Committee appointments and Board elections.
  • Budget approval (applicable post-incorporation).

Every vote is published in the minutes with the tally.
