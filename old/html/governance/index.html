Governance index

Two frameworks. One chain to secure. Transparent by default.

The Council's governance output is narrow on purpose: the Network Governance Framework (the constitutional layer every EGF on Verana must respect) and the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework (the rules for the four Essential Credential Schemas). Everything the Council does is an amendment, a vote, or a sanction under one of these two documents, all on public record.

How amendments work

  1. Proposal. Any Member may submit a proposed amendment to either framework via the responsible committee (Network GF Committee or ECS-EGF Committee).
  2. Committee review. The committee drafts the amendment text and publishes it for Member comment (minimum 30 days).
  3. Member comment period. Open to all Members and Observers. Comments and committee responses are published.
  4. General Assembly vote. Adopted at the next regular or extraordinary General Assembly per the threshold set in the Bylaws.
  5. Publication. The adopted amendment is published here with its effective date, the vote tally, and the full diff against the previous version.

All amendment history is kept in the public record. No silent changes.
