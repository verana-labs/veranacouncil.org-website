Legal disclosure

Legal entities.

Verana's institutional work is split across two legal entities operating under separate jurisdictions and charters. This page discloses both.

Verana Council Association

  • Type: Swiss Verein (non-profit association), Art. 60–79 ZGB.
  • Jurisdiction: Switzerland. Seat: [SEAT_CITY], Canton of [CANTON].
  • Status: consortium-in-formation. Target incorporation: Q3 2026.
  • Registry reference: to be assigned at incorporation (Swiss Commercial Registry).
  • Remit: authors and operates the Verana Network Governance Framework and the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework; secures the Verana chain via Member validators.
  • Website: this site (veranacouncil.org).

Verana Foundation

  • Type: Cayman Foundation Company.
  • Jurisdiction: Cayman Islands.
  • Status: to be established.
  • Remit: treasury management, developer grants, ecosystem programs. Separate and independent from the Council.
  • Website: verana.foundation (post-incorporation).

Relationship

The Council and the Foundation are legally distinct. The Foundation does not direct Council governance, and the Council does not direct Foundation treasury or grant decisions. Cross-entity coordination happens through published memoranda; all are subject to conflict-of-interest rules in both entities.

Pre-formation acts

Until Q3 2026, the Council's pre-formation acts are executed by the Founding Steward in personal capacity. At incorporation, pre-formation acts transfer to the Association by General Assembly resolution.
