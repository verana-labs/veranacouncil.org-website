Observer track

Compatible with public-sector procurement.

Many sovereign governments, multilateral bodies, and state-chartered public agencies cannot sign Swiss Verein membership agreements under their procurement or sovereign-immunity rules, yet recognize the strategic value of engaging with the Council. The Public-Sector Observer tier is designed for this case: non-voting, no Verein membership, compatible with public-sector constraints, and convertible to voting membership when and if procurement permits.

Apply

What Observer status provides

  • Advance notice of Network GF and ECS-EGF drafts that touch public-sector credential schemas (ECS-Persona, ECS-Organization).
  • Participation in open-session Council meetings. Observers may speak in open session by Board invitation.
  • Formal endorsement via Memorandum of Understanding. An MoU between the public body and the Council affirms the body's recognition of Verana infrastructure; MoU text is published.
  • Access to the Standards Liaison Committee. Public-sector Observers frequently have existing relationships with W3C, DIF, and OIDF that the Committee leverages.

What Observer status does NOT provide

  • Voting rights at the General Assembly.
  • Frameworks authorship rights (Observers may comment; Members author and vote).
  • ECS Issuer or Issuer-Grantor status. Public-sector bodies seeking ECS accreditation apply separately under the ECS-EGF.
  • Validator operation. The validator set is restricted to voting Council Members.

Compatibility with Member status

Observer status does not count against the (sector × region) seat-diversity rule. A sovereign Observer and a voting Council Member from the same cell may coexist. This makes Observer status a working on-ramp: a public body that later clears procurement can convert to voting membership without displacing an existing Member.

Application process

  1. Expression of interest. /join/apply?track=observer (a 2-minute form).
  2. MoU draft. Standard MoU template shared; reviewable by the body's legal authority.
  3. Countersignature. MoU countersigned by the Founding Steward (pre-incorporation) or by the Board (post-incorporation).
  4. Publication. MoU text is published on the Observer's profile page at /members.

Precedent

The tier is modeled on the Hedera Council's 2025 Strategic Partnership program, adapted for the Swiss Verein context. Observer status can be a permanent associate status or a step toward full voting membership.
