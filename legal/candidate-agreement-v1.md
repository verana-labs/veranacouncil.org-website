## 1. Purpose

The Candidate applies for the open Council seat **{{seat_label}}** and, if
admitted, intends to serve as an Initial Council Member of the Verana Council:
the sole body that governs and secures the live Verana network.

## 2. Candidacy and admission

2.1 This Agreement records a candidacy. It does **not** admit the Candidate to
the Council, confer any seat, voting right, governance right, or validator
right. Admission requires an admission ballot: one ballot per candidate,
accept or refuse, decided by a two-thirds (⅔) supermajority of the seated
Council Members, under the Council's provisional pre-mainnet admission
mechanism.

2.2 Candidacies for the same seat are balloted one at a time, in order of
completed candidacy. If another candidate is seated first, this candidacy
lapses; the Candidate may re-sign for another open seat or stand by as a
successor candidate.

2.3 The provisional admission mechanism exists solely to assemble the initial
Council before mainnet launch. At mainnet launch it sunsets and all Council
voting, including admissions, is exercised on-chain via validator nodes.

2.4 All pre-incorporation seatings are subject to en-bloc ratification at the
constitutive General Assembly of the Verein.

## 3. Membership terms (upon admission)

3.1 **Free membership.** No dues and no capital contribution are payable. The
statutes of the Verein shall exclude personal financial liability of members
(Art. 75a ZGB).

3.2 **One status.** Upon admission and incorporation, the Candidate becomes a
statutory member of the Verein, a General Assembly voter (one member, one
vote), and a validator operator.

3.3 **Validator obligation.** Operating a validator node on the Verana mainnet
is a mandatory and inseparable component of Council membership from mainnet
launch; voting rights are exercised exclusively via an active validator node.
During the formation period the Candidate **should** operate a validator node
on the Verana testnet as the readiness step for the genesis validator set.
Validator running costs are borne by the member and offset, after launch, by
on-chain validator rewards.

3.4 **Seat terms.** Validator terms are fixed, with formal renewal.

## 4. Public record

The Candidate acknowledges that the candidacy, the admission ballot and its
outcome, and any seating are published on the Council's public record.
Personal data of representatives is not published.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information obtained from
the other in connection with this Agreement. This Section survives
termination.

## 6. Term and termination

This Agreement remains in effect until the candidacy is decided, lapses, or is
withdrawn, or upon execution of the Council Membership Agreement, whichever
occurs first.
