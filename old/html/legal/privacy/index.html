Privacy notice

Privacy.

The Verana Council Association is established in Switzerland and adheres to the EU GDPR and Swiss FADP for the processing of personal data of site visitors, applicants, Members, and Observers.

Last updated: 2026-04-30

Data controller

The Verana Council Association (consortium-in-formation pre-Q3 2026). Post-incorporation, controller reference published in the Legal entities page.

What we collect

  • Application data. Name, organization, title, email, region, sector, and free-text rationale submitted via /join/apply. Used solely to process the application.
  • Contact data. Name, email, outlet, topic submitted via /contact. Used solely to respond.
  • Analytics. If you consent via the cookie banner: Plausible (self-hosted, IP-anonymized) page views. No cross-site tracking.

What we do not collect

  • No third-party marketing pixels.
  • No advertising cookies.
  • No YouTube embeds or social-media tracking on this site.

Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Applications and contact forms: Art. 6(1)(b): performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request.
  • Analytics: Art. 6(1)(a): consent (revocable at any time).

Retention

Application data is retained for the lifetime of the application process plus 24 months for audit and non-discrimination review. Non-successful applications are anonymized after this period.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Swiss FDPIC or your local EU DPA. Write to privacy@verana.io.

International transfers

Personal data is stored in Switzerland and the EEA. No transfers outside these jurisdictions without an adequate safeguard (SCCs or adequacy decision).

Changes to this notice

Material changes are announced on News and reflected here with an updated last-updated date.
