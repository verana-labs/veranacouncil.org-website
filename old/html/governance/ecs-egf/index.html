The ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework.

The Council's own ecosystem-level framework. It covers the four fixed Essential Credential Schemas (Service, Organization, Persona, UserAgent) and the rules under which the Council selects Issuer Grantors and Issuers for each schema in each jurisdiction.

How the Council selects Issuer Grantors and Issuers

For schemas in GRANTOR_VALIDATION_PROCESS mode (currently ECS-ORG and ECS-PERSONA):

  1. The Council opens a call for Issuer Grantor applications in a specific (schema × jurisdiction) cell.
  2. Applications are reviewed by the ECS-EGF Committee against published criteria (legal standing, operational capacity, jurisdiction fit, track record).
  3. The Committee's recommendation is voted at the General Assembly (threshold per Bylaws).
  4. Selected Issuer Grantors accredit downstream Issuers per their own published criteria, subject to the ECS-EGF.

For schemas in ECOSYSTEM_VALIDATION_PROCESS mode (currently ECS-SERVICE): the Council (as Ecosystem controller) accredits Issuers directly, via the same Committee-recommendation / GA-vote flow.

For schemas in OPEN mode (currently ECS-UA): no accreditation gate. Any participant meeting the on-chain schema requirements may issue.

Key principle

A Council seat is a governance vote on the Council's own frameworks. It does NOT by itself confer Issuer or Issuer-Grantor status. ECS accreditation is a separate process, granted under the ECS-EGF. Some Council Members will seek ECS accreditation; others will not. Neither role implies the other.

Full text

The full normative text of the ECS-EGF is maintained at github.com/verana-labs/verana-council-gov/ecs-egf. Changes tracked via git; each published version has a permanent URL and a SRI hash.
