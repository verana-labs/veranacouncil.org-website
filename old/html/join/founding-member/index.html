Founding Member track

Founding Members write the rules. Later members live by them.

The Verana Council Association Swiss Verein incorporates in Q3 2026. Until then, Founding Members are signing non-binding Letters of Intent and co-authoring the Bylaws, the Network GF, and the ECS-EGF. At incorporation, LOIs ratify to Membership Agreements.

What a Founding seat gives your organization

  1. Authorship of the Verana Network Governance Framework: Co-author the network's constitutional layer: the rules every EGF on Verana must respect, the graduated risk-management rubric, the dispute-resolution process, the chain-evolution process. The Network GF is not an approval gate for sector ecosystems; it is the set of requirements every EGF must satisfy.
  2. Authorship of the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework: As Ecosystem controller of the ECS Trust Registry, co-author the ECS-EGF: the four fixed schemas (Service, Organization, Persona, UserAgent), the permission-management mode for each, and the rules under which the Council selects Issuer Grantors and Issuers per (schema × jurisdiction).
  3. Verein bylaws and seat-structure co-authorship: Until Q3 2026 the Verein statutes, seat-diversity rules, and Code of Conduct are still being written. Founding Members co-author them.
  4. Voting seat at the General Assembly: One vote per Member, on every Council matter: framework amendments, ECS accreditations, sanctions at Suspension / Slashing rubric steps, Board elections.

A Council seat is a governance vote on the Council's own frameworks. It does not by itself confer Issuer or Issuer-Grantor status, and the Council does not approve sector EGFs. A Member is not required to author an EGF, and an EGF lead-author is not required to hold a Council seat.

What we expect from Founding Members

  • One voice per (sector × region). Diversity is a hard constraint.
  • Governance participation. Quarterly General Assembly attendance, voting, and participation in at least one committee (Network GF, ECS-EGF, Risk & Sanctions, Dispute Resolution, Validator Operations, Standards Liaison, Nominations & Conduct).
  • Validator operation after mainnet, either directly or via a qualified delegated operator under the Member's accountability. Only Members run validators. Failure to maintain a healthy validator (uptime, slashing-clean record) is reviewed under the Code of Conduct.
  • Code of Conduct adherence including the conflict-of-interest cooldown (members recuse on decisions affecting direct competitors).

Application process

  1. Submit an expression of interest. /join/apply?track=founding-member (a 2-minute form).
  2. 20-minute intro call with the Founding Steward. We align on seat fit, sector, and jurisdiction.
  3. Review non-binding LOI. Standard template; your General Counsel reviews draft Verein statutes before Q3 ratification.
  4. Sign LOI. Countersigned by the Founding Steward on behalf of the consortium-in-formation.
  5. Ratification at Verein incorporation (Q3 2026). Your LOI converts to a formal Membership Agreement.

Frequently asked questions

"We are a multinational. Which regional cell applies?"

The cell tracks your organization's operational regional centre of gravity, not your legal headquarters. The Nominations & Conduct Committee confirms fit. Adjacent cells are discussed if the primary cell is contested.

"We operate in multiple sectors. Which applies?"

Nominate the sector you most strongly represent in your application. The Committee confirms. Second-sector interest can be expressed via a complementary Observer role (non-voting).

"Our General Counsel needs to review the LOI. Can we get the current draft?"

Yes. Send your interest via /join/apply?track=founding-member and the LOI draft is shared under NDA on request.

"We cannot run a validator. Does that disqualify us?"

Not necessarily. The Member's accountability for a qualified delegated operator satisfies the obligation. Academic institutions, in particular, often prefer delegation. Discuss in the intro call.

"We have a direct competitor also applying. What happens?"

Both LOIs are reviewed. The Nominations & Conduct Committee recommends one for the voting seat based on published criteria. The non-selected applicant is invited to the next recruitment window or to an adjacent cell.
