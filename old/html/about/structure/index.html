Association structure

A Swiss Verein with operational obligations.

The Verana Council Association is being established as a non-profit Swiss Verein under Art. 60 ZGB. Target incorporation: Q3 2026. Every Member has both a governance vote and an operational obligation: running a validator node on the Verana chain.

Legal form

  • Type: Swiss Verein (association), Art. 60–79 ZGB.
  • Jurisdiction: Switzerland. Seat: [SEAT_CITY], Canton of [CANTON].
  • Incorporation: target Q3 2026.
  • Purpose (draft, to be ratified at incorporation): to author, operate, and evolve the governance frameworks of the Verana network; to secure and operate the chain; to maintain the Code of Conduct.
  • Organs: General Assembly (all Members), Board, Committees (see below). Every Member has one vote at the General Assembly, irrespective of size.
  • Profit distribution: none. Non-profit by statute.

Member obligations

  • Governance participation. Attend the quarterly General Assembly. Vote on Network GF and ECS-EGF amendments. Participate in at least one committee.
  • Validator operation. Operate a validator node on the Verana chain, either directly or via a qualified delegated operator under the Member's accountability. The validator set is restricted to Council Members; only Members can run a validator node. Failure to maintain a healthy validator (uptime, slashing-clean record) is reviewed under the Code of Conduct.
  • Code of Conduct adherence. Including the conflict-of-interest cooldown (recusal on decisions affecting direct competitors) and the seat-diversity rule.

Board

The Board is elected by and from the Members at the General Assembly. Board Members serve two-year staggered terms to provide continuity. Board composition is published and regularly refreshed through elections.

Initial Board composition (effective from incorporation): [TO_BE_ELECTED_AT_Q3_2026_GA].

Committees

The Council's work runs through standing committees, each with a published charter:

  • Network GF Committee: drafts and amends the Network Governance Framework.
  • ECS-EGF Committee: drafts and amends the ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework.
  • Risk & Sanctions Committee: operates the graduated risk-management rubric and sanctions process.
  • Dispute Resolution Panel: handles disputes escalated under the Network GF.
  • Validator Operations Committee: sets operational standards for Member validator nodes and reviews performance.
  • Standards Liaison Committee: coordinates with W3C, DIF, Hyperledger, OpenWallet, OIDF, and ToIP.
  • Nominations & Conduct Committee: oversees seat recruitment and enforces the Code of Conduct.

Ad-hoc committees may be established by General Assembly resolution.

Founding Steward (pre-incorporation)

Until Q3 2026, the Council is a consortium-in-formation. Pre-incorporation activity is administered by the Founding Steward, a collective role held in personal capacity by Fabrice Rochette, Ariel Gentile, Philip Bildner, and David Rennie. At incorporation, the Founding Steward's pre-formation acts transfer to the Association and the Steward role dissolves.
