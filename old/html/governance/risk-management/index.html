Sanctions rubric

Graduated sanctions, uniform rubric, public record.

The Council enforces participant conduct via a graduated sanctions rubric defined in the Network GF. Every sanction, from a first-notice warning through a slashing event, is public record, cross-referenced to the triggering conduct, the sanction step applied, and the participant's right of appeal.

The graduated-sanctions ladder

  1. 01

    Notice

    Participant notified of issue; remediation window opens.

  2. 02

    Remediation

    Participant required to remediate within defined window.

  3. 03

    Suspension

    Permission suspended pending remediation or appeal.

  4. 04

    Slashing

    Trust-deposit slashed per schedule; permission revoked.

Every step is reviewable; every step has a published procedure in the Network GF.

Who applies it

  • Risk & Sanctions Committee: standing committee of the Council. Reviews incidents, proposes step-ups, escalates to the General Assembly on slashing decisions.
  • Dispute Resolution Panel: hears appeals from affected participants.
  • General Assembly: final authority on slashing events and on any sanction affecting a Member or Observer.

Scope

The rubric applies uniformly across:

  • ECS Issuer Grantors and Issuers (under the ECS-EGF)
  • Any participant operating under a sector EGF on Verana (per the EGF's conformance with the Network GF)
  • Council Members (under the Code of Conduct and validator operational standards)

Public record

Every sanction decision is published on News with:

  • The triggering conduct (redacted only where legally required).
  • The rubric step applied.
  • The participant's response and remediation (if applicable).
  • The appeal status (if any).
  • The vote tally (for slashing events and General Assembly actions).
