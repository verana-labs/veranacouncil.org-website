Behavioral floor

Binding on every Member. Binding on every Observer. Binding on the Board.

The Code of Conduct is the behavioral floor of the Verana Council. It applies to every Member, every Public-Sector Observer, every committee participant, and every staff member of the Association.

Scope

The Code governs conduct in:

  • General Assembly sessions (open, Member, and closed).
  • Committee meetings and committee work.
  • Public communications where the participant speaks as a Council representative.
  • Validator node operation (under the Validator Operations standards).
  • Dispute Resolution Panel proceedings.

It does not govern:

  • The participant's internal organizational conduct unrelated to the Council.
  • Commercial relationships between Members outside of Council work.
  • Social or personal conduct unrelated to Council representation.

Core provisions (draft, to be ratified Q3 2026)

  1. Integrity. Members and Observers act with honesty in all Council matters. Deliberate misrepresentation in applications, votes, or committee work is grounds for sanction.
  2. Conflict-of-interest cooldown. Members recuse themselves from votes on matters that directly benefit or disadvantage a competitor, a controlled entity, or a commercial counterparty. Disclosure is required upfront; recusal is required in the vote itself.
  3. Confidentiality. Member-session and closed-session content is not disclosed outside the session without authorization. Breach is grounds for sanction up to suspension of seat.
  4. Validator operational standards. Members maintain a healthy validator: uptime targets, slashing-clean record, key-management practices, and timely upgrade response. Failure is reviewed under the graduated-sanctions rubric.
  5. Non-discrimination. No Council participant may discriminate on the basis of nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or political affiliation in Council work.
  6. No harassment. Harassment, intimidation, or retaliation is prohibited. Reports are handled by the Nominations & Conduct Committee; anonymity of reporter is preserved where procedurally possible.
  7. Public statements. Members do not speak on behalf of the Council without authorization. Members may speak as their own organization on Council matters, provided they clearly identify their role.
  8. No buying influence. No gifts, no undisclosed payments, no side-agreements that compromise a Member's independence. Hospitality at Council meetings is governed by a published hospitality policy.

Enforcement

Violations are reviewed by the Nominations & Conduct Committee under the graduated-sanctions rubric. Serious violations may be referred directly to the General Assembly.

Amendment

The Code of Conduct is amended by the same process as the Bylaws.
