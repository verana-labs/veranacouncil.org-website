Panel procedure

Public process, written procedure, right of appeal.

The Network GF defines a single dispute-resolution process for the whole network. The Dispute Resolution Panel is a standing Council committee that hears disputes between participants, between a participant and an EGF operator, or between an EGF operator and the Council itself.

What can be disputed

  • A sanction decision under the graduated-sanctions rubric.
  • A denial or revocation of ECS Issuer or Issuer-Grantor accreditation.
  • An EGF operator's decision against a participant (where the EGF has delegated appeal to the Council).
  • Conduct alleged to violate the Network GF or the Code of Conduct.

What cannot be disputed here

  • Sector-EGF-internal decisions where the EGF has its own appeal process. The Council's venue is only available if the EGF explicitly delegates.
  • Commercial disputes between vendors running on Verana. Those are out of scope.
  • Technical bug reports. Those go to the relevant repository on github.com/verana-labs.

Process

  1. Filing. Written complaint submitted via /contact?topic=dispute or by mail to the Association's registered seat.
  2. Standing & timeliness review. The Panel reviews whether the complaint is admissible (standing, timeliness, scope).
  3. Hearing. Written submissions from both sides. Oral hearing by Panel discretion.
  4. Decision. Written decision with reasons, published on News within 30 days of the hearing.
  5. Appeal. Decisions may be appealed to the General Assembly within 60 days. GA rulings are final within the Association's authority.

Venue and governing law

  • Venue: Verana Council Association seat, [CANTON], Switzerland.
  • Governing law: Swiss law, per the Association's bylaws.
  • Confidentiality: hearings are presumptively public; the Panel may redact submissions only for commercially-sensitive or legally-privileged content, with reasons.
