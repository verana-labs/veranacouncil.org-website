## **RECITALS**

**WHEREAS**, the Verana Foundation (the **“Foundation”**), a prospective non-profit entity to be incorporated under the laws of a jurisdiction yet to be determined, is responsible for the organization, stewardship, and pre-mainnet administration of the decentralized trust infrastructure known as the **Verana Network**, including its Essential Credential Schemas (“ECSs”) Ecosystem, schemas for organizational identity and credential authority, testnet governance structures, and the preparation of an Verana ECS Ecosystem Governance Framework (“EGF”) to be adopted upon mainnet launch;

**WHEREAS**, the Verana Network is intended to enable decentralized, interoperable, cryptographically decentralized trust network ecosystems based at least on decentralized identifiers (DIDs), verifiable credentials, trust registries;

**WHEREAS**, upon formal incorporation of the Foundation and launch of the Verana Network mainnet, the **Verana Council** shall be constituted as the governance authority having full, exclusive, and plenary oversight of the Verana ECS Ecosystem, including, without limitation: (a) the approval, amendment, or deprecation of ECSs; (b) the accreditation of **Issuer Grantors** responsible for selecting and supervising Issuers; (c) the accreditation of **Issuers** authorized to issue Verifiable Credentials in conformity with approved ECSs; (d) the governance of the Verana ECS Ecosystem in accordance with the EGF; and (e) the maintenance and oversight of the relevant Trust Registry components;

**WHEREAS**, prior to mainnet launch, and for the limited purpose of training, evaluation, operational testing, readiness assessment, schema-governance simulation, and issuance simulation, the Foundation shall instantiate and administer a provisional **Testnet ECS Ecosystem**, which shall have no production status, no binding governance effect, and no bearing on the rights, duties, or liabilities that will apply upon adoption of the EGF;

**WHEREAS**, entities wishing to be considered as potential Issuer Grantors and/or Issuers for the mainnet Verana ECS Ecosystem must first undergo a program of provisional participation, assessment, and evaluation within the Testnet ECS Ecosystem, and must demonstrate technical readiness, operational discipline, governance comprehension, audit responsiveness, issuance accuracy, schema-governance aptitude, and trust-registry participation capability sufficient to merit consideration by the Verana Council after mainnet launch;

**WHEREAS**, the entity executing this MoU (the **“Participant”**) desires to participate in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem solely as a **Participant Candidate**, for the purpose of being **evaluated and pre-selected** for potential mainnet accreditation as:

(a) an **Issuer Grantor Candidate**, provisionally capable of evaluating, selecting, and supervising Issuers under one or more ECSs of the Verana ECS Ecosystem;

(b) an **Issuer Candidate**, provisionally capable of issuing Verifiable Credentials under one or more ECSs of the Verana ECS Ecosystem;

or (c) both roles, if approved by the Verana Council;

**WHEREAS**, the Foundation is willing, on a **non-binding basis** except as expressly provided herein, to facilitate the Participant’s provisional, testnet-only involvement in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem, including access to governance simulations, issuance testing, schema review processes, provisional audit procedures, and other readiness-evaluation mechanisms, all for the exclusive purpose of informing the Verana Council’s future assessment following mainnet launch;

# **SECTION 1**

DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Memorandum of Understanding (this **“MoU”**), and for all interpretive, operational, evaluative, and compliance purposes arising in connection herewith, the following capitalized terms shall have the respective meanings set forth below. Each definition shall apply equally to the singular and plural forms of the defined terms, and grammatical variants thereof shall be construed accordingly.

1.4 **“ECS”** or **“Essential Credential Schema”** means any credential schema designated by the Verana Council as foundational to the Verana ECS Ecosystem and necessary for establishing baseline trust, organizational identity, issuance authority, governance functionality, schema validation, ecosystem membership, or similar activities integral to the operation of decentralized credential ecosystems. ECSs include, without limitation, organizational identity schemas, verifiable service schema, verifiable user agent schema, persona schema, and such other essential schemas as may be approved by the Verana Council from time to time.

1.5 **“Issuer”** means a Participant Candidate that seeks or is being evaluated for the potential future role of issuing Verifiable Credentials under one or more ECSs, subject always to mainnet accreditation by the Verana Council under the EGF.

1.6 **“Issuer Candidate”** means a Participant engaged in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem for the purpose of being evaluated for potential mainnet selection as an Issuer.

1.7 **“Issuer Grantor”** means a Participant Candidate that seeks or is being evaluated for the potential future role of approving, accrediting, designating, supervising, and managing Issuers of one or more ECSs within the mainnet Verana ECS Ecosystem, subject always to mainnet accreditation by the Verana Council under the EGF.

1.8 **“Issuer Grantor Candidate”** means a Participant engaged in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem for the purpose of being evaluated for potential mainnet selection as an Issuer Grantor.

1.9 **“Participant”** or **“Participant Candidate”** means the entity executing this MoU for the sole purpose of being evaluated for potential pre-selection as an Issuer Candidate or Issuer Grantor Candidate for the Verana ECS Ecosystem upon mainnet launch. 1.16 **“Working Draft”** means any provisional, preliminary, experimental, or draft governance document, schema draft, credential attribute definition, evaluation rubric, procedural outline, simulated audit rule, or any other non-final ECS-related documentation furnished by the Foundation, which shall be treated as Confidential Information unless and until made public.

# **SECTION 2**

NATURE OF THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

AND RELATIONSHIP TO MAINNET GOVERNANCE

2.1 **Non-Binding Instrument of Intent; Absence of Present Legal Commitments Except as Expressly Stated.**

This Memorandum of Understanding (this **“MoU”**) constitutes a non-binding instrument of mutual intent between the Parties for the limited and provisional purpose of facilitating the Participant’s engagement in, and evaluation under, the Verana ECS Ecosystem during its pre-mainnet, testnet-only operational phase. Except for those provisions expressly designated herein as binding pursuant to Section 2.4, nothing contained in this MoU, nor any action taken pursuant hereto, shall be construed to create or impose upon either Party any legally enforceable obligation to: (a) select or approve the Participant, whether as an Issuer Grantor or as an Issuer, for the mainnet Verana ECS Ecosystem; (b) confer or imply any authority, right, entitlement, or privilege with respect to the production Verana Network following its mainnet launch; (c) enter into any future agreement or binding arrangement; or (d) continue any relationship established pursuant to this MoU beyond the termination provisions set forth herein.

2.2 **Pre-Mainnet Purpose and Provisional Status.**

The Participant expressly acknowledges and agrees that the sole purpose of this MoU is to define the terms upon which the Participant may, on an entirely provisional basis, participate in the **Verana ECS Ecosystem Testnet** for evaluation, training, simulation, credential-issuance testing, schema-governance familiarization, operational readiness assessment, and related exploratory activities. Any and all provisional authorizations conferred during such participation shall be limited to the testnet environment and shall confer **no authority, right, permission, standing, or recognition whatsoever** with respect to the Verana Network’s production mainnet environment or any governance structure associated therewith.

2.3 **Supersession by the Ecosystem Governance Framework (“EGF”).**

The Participant acknowledges that the Verana Council, upon its full establishment at mainnet launch, shall promulgate, adopt, and enforce a comprehensive **Ecosystem Governance Framework (“EGF”)** applicable to the Verana ECS Ecosystem. Upon the EGF’s publication and effective date, this MoU shall, **without further action of either Party**, be automatically, immediately, and irrevocably superseded in its entirety. From that moment forward: (a) all rights, obligations, roles, and governance structures associated with Issuer Grantors, Issuers, schema authorities, audit requirements, compliance obligations, revocation responsibilities, trust-registry participation, assurance levels, or any other ECS-related matter shall be governed exclusively by the EGF; (b) no term, obligation, expectation, or representation contained in this MoU shall survive except as expressly designated in Sections 6 through 12; and (c) the Participant shall not rely on any provision of this MoU for mainnet participation.

2.4 **Binding Portions of this MoU.**

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Participant acknowledges and agrees that the following provisions shall constitute legally binding and enforceable obligations of the Parties from the Effective Date until such obligations expire or are superseded according to their terms: **Section 6 (Intellectual Property Rights); Section 7 (Confidentiality); Section 8 (Restrictions); Section 9 (Disclaimers and No Warranties); Section 10 (Governing Law); Section 11 (Dispute Resolution); Section 12 (Term and Termination), together with any survival clauses therein.** All remaining provisions of this MoU shall be interpreted solely as non-binding expressions of mutual intent, without the creation of any enforceable rights, duties, causes of action, remedies, or liabilities.

2.5 **No Guarantee of Selection for Mainnet Participation.**

Nothing in this MoU—whether by implication, course of dealing, or reliance—shall be construed as creating any commitment or assurance that the Participant will be selected or approved by the Verana Council as an Issuer Grantor or as an Issuer for mainnet deployment. The Participant understands and agrees that (a) selection for mainnet roles shall lie exclusively within the authority of the Verana Council; (b) the Council may apply, revise, or expand selection criteria; (c) the Council may decline to select the Participant for any reason or for no reason; and (d) participation in the testnet environment is undertaken entirely at the Participant’s own discretion and risk, without inducement, guarantee, or expectation of advancement.

2.6 **No Fiduciary Relationship; No Partnership or Agency.**

This MoU does not create, and shall not be construed as creating, any partnership, joint venture, franchise, association, representation, delegation, or agency relationship between the Parties; nor does it impose any fiduciary duty or special duty of care upon either Party. The Participant shall have no authority to bind, obligate, or speak on behalf of the Foundation, and shall not represent itself as possessing such authority.

2.7 **Provisional Governance Interpretation.**

Any governance guidance informally provided during the testnet—including, without limitation, draft ECS documentation, preliminary governance models, experimental schema behaviors, provisional issuer accreditation rules, provisional audit processes, or preliminary ecosystem role definitions—shall not be interpreted as binding or final. The Participant acknowledges that the Foundation may alter, revise, retract, or replace any such provisional guidance without notice, and that the EGF shall constitute the definitive and exclusive governing instrument.

2.8 **No Reliance for Mainnet Purposes.**

The Participant expressly agrees that it shall not rely upon this MoU or any communications, permissions, provisional authorizations, or informal indications of suitability or readiness for the purpose of:

(a) marketing itself to third parties as a future Issuer or Issuer Grantor;

(b) representing to third parties that the Foundation or the Verana Council has approved or intends to approve the Participant;

(c) establishing commercial expectations or agreements predicated upon presumed mainnet authorization.

2.9 **Interpretation Against Reliance.**

The Parties intend that this MoU shall be interpreted to preclude claims of detrimental reliance, promissory estoppel, implied contract, or any comparable legal doctrine. The Participant undertakes all testnet activities with the understanding that they do not create reliance interests or expectations of mainnet roles.

2.10 **Survival of Binding Clauses.**

All binding provisions identified in Section 2.4 shall survive:

(a) the adoption of the EGF;

(b) the supersession of this MoU;

(c) the expiration or termination of this MoU; and

(d) any decision of the Verana Council not to approve the Participant for mainnet participation.

# **SECTION 3**

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTICIPANT CANDIDATES

3.1 **General Nature of Participant Roles.**

The Participant acknowledges and agrees that, by executing this MoU, it seeks only provisional, pre-mainnet consideration as a potential Issuer Grantor Candidate and/or Issuer Candidate (each as defined herein). The Participant further understands that such consideration occurs solely within the Testnet ECS Ecosystem and does not, in and of itself, confer or imply any authority, role, designation, accreditation, operational mandate, governance position, trust registry permission, issuance capacity, or schema-authority status within any mainnet ecosystem or within any production instance of the Verana Network. All roles described in this Section 3 are preliminary, provisional, revocable, and wholly subordinate to future determinations rendered by the Verana Council pursuant to the EGF.

3.2 **Issuer Grantor Candidate Role.**

To the extent the Participant indicates its intention to be evaluated as an Issuer Grantor Candidate, the Participant acknowledges that the Issuer Grantor role, upon mainnet launch, shall entail responsibility for schema governance and the approval of Issuers under designated ECSs. Accordingly, during the testnet phase, the Participant may be evaluated on its ability to:

(a) comprehend the conceptual, structural, and operational principles of ECSs, including schema evolution processes, schema compliance boundaries, and schema-governance mandates;

(b) engage in credential-schema review, analysis, critique, or refinement activities, whether informally or as part of designated schema-governance simulations;

(c) assess prospective Issuers’ operational readiness, governance discipline, credential accuracy, and procedural integrity;

(d) apply reasoned judgment in evaluating whether a prospective Issuer is fit to issue credentials under a specific ECS;

(e) adhere to provisional guidelines governing Issuer Grantor conduct, evaluation methodologies, delegation practices, conflict-of-interest avoidance, and schema-approval participation;

(f) demonstrate awareness of risk factors associated with credential issuance, schema misuse, mis-issuance, revocation responsibilities, and downstream trust implications within decentralized trust networks;

(g) participate constructively in schema-related governance simulations, including feedback loops, multi-stakeholder deliberations, or role-based exercises conducted under the Testnet ECS Ecosystem.

3.3 **Issuer Candidate Role.**

To the extent the Participant indicates its intention to be evaluated as an Issuer Candidate, the Participant understands that, upon mainnet launch, Issuers shall hold responsibility for issuing Verifiable Credentials under specific ECSs that may be relied upon by third parties and Ecosystem participants. Accordingly, during the testnet phase, the Participant may be evaluated on its ability to:

(a) accurately implement credential issuance workflows in alignment with ECS specifications, data models, contextual integrity requirements, and schema-defined semantics;

(b) correctly and reliably record issuance events in the Testnet ECS Trust Registry, including issuance metadata, revocation events, issuance timestamps, assurance levels, and any other fields designated for testnet compliance;

(c) observe provisional audit, compliance, and operational requirements, including mandatory audits (annual, random, or risk-based), human-review processes for high-assurance credential types, and any provisional quality or consistency checks established by the Foundation;

(d) correct mis-issued testnet credentials promptly, participate in failure-analysis exercises, and support remediation or revocation workflows simulating real-world credential governance scenarios;

(e) uphold provisional operational standards, including credential issuance discipline, reliability, responsiveness, and adherence to governance and compliance signals issued by the Foundation or, post-launch, the Verana Council;

(f) demonstrate comprehension of risks associated with credential issuance and the trust dependencies created by published credentials, including risks to individuals, entities, ecosystems, and the broader Verana Network.

3.4 **Selection of Roles by Participant; No Obligation for Dual Consideration.**

The Participant may request evaluation as an Issuer Grantor Candidate, an Issuer Candidate, or both, as indicated in the signature block hereto. The Foundation shall have no obligation to evaluate the Participant for any role other than the one(s) expressly selected, nor any obligation to evaluate the Participant for a broader scope of trust responsibilities. Notwithstanding such selection, the Foundation may, based on its own assessment of the Participant’s demonstrated capabilities, choose to evaluate the Participant for fewer or additional provisional roles.

3.5 **Provisional Authorization; No Presumption of Competence.**

Any provisional authorization granted to the Participant within the Testnet ECS Ecosystem—including authorization to simulate issuance, record mock issuance events, or participate in credential schema evaluations—shall:

(a) be revocable at any time and without notice;

(b) confer no presumption of technical, operational, or governance competence beyond the confines of the testnet;

(c) confer no rights, entitlements, or expectations for mainnet; and

(d) exist solely for the purpose of enabling the Foundation to evaluate the Participant’s readiness to function under the stricter requirements of the EGF.

3.6 **Participant Duties During Testnet ECS Activities.**

During all testnet activities, the Participant shall:

(a) participate in good faith and with professional diligence;

(b) refrain from engaging in any conduct that could disrupt, degrade, compromise, or impair the Testnet ECS Ecosystem or the Foundation’s governance processes;

(c) comply with all provisional guidelines, instructions, and directives communicated by the Foundation;

(d) provide accurate and non-harmful test data;

(e) cooperate fully with audits and evaluations;

(f) abide by provisional revocation or correction procedures;

(g) respect all confidentiality restrictions in Section 7\.

3.7 **No Authority to Bind Foundation or Verana Council.**

The Participant shall not, by virtue of this MoU or its participation in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem, acquire any authority to bind, obligate, represent, or purport to represent the Foundation or the Verana Council in any capacity or to issue any credential purporting to have official standing or recognition beyond the testnet environment.

3.8 **Interpretation of This Section in Favor of Provisionality.**

For the avoidance of doubt, all provisions of this Section 3 shall be interpreted in a manner maximizing their provisional, pre-mainnet, evaluative character. Nothing in this Section shall be construed to establish rights or obligations applicable to the mainnet Verana ECS Ecosystem, which shall be governed exclusively by the EGF.

# **SECTION 4**

TESTNET PARTICIPATION AND PRE-SELECTION EVALUATION

4.1 **Purpose and Limited Scope of Testnet Participation.**

The Participant expressly acknowledges and agrees that the **Verana ECS Ecosystem Testnet** (the **“Testnet ECS Ecosystem”**) constitutes a non-production, pre-mainnet, provisional operational environment established and administered solely for the purposes of: (a) simulating credential governance processes; (b) facilitating technical and procedural experimentation; (c) enabling preliminary schema-governance engagement; (d) permitting demonstration of issuance capabilities; (e) evaluating operational readiness; (f) assessing compliance behaviors and audit responsiveness; and (g) supporting the Foundation’s formulation of recommendations to the Verana Council regarding potential mainnet participant eligibility. No data, issuance activity, operational result, or behavior occurring within the Testnet ECS Ecosystem shall carry any legal standing, evidentiary status, trust significance, or reputational implication beyond the confines of this provisional environment.

4.2 **Provisional Authorization and Revocability.**

Any authorization granted by the Foundation to the Participant during the testnet phase—whether involving issuance of test credentials, participation in schema-governance simulations, execution of testnet schema authority responsibilities, or interaction with trust registry components—shall be strictly provisional, revocable, and subject to adjustment or withdrawal at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice. The Participant acknowledges and agrees that such provisional authorization:

(a) does not constitute any formal appointment, accreditation, delegation, recognition, or designation applicable to the mainnet Verana ECS Ecosystem;

(b) does not create any reliance interest or expectation of continuation;

(c) confers no rights, privileges, or benefits beyond the testnet;

(d) shall not be construed as evidencing the Participant’s fitness for mainnet participation, which is exclusively determined by the Verana Council pursuant to the EGF.

4.3 **Audit, Assessment, and Compliance Simulation.**

The Participant acknowledges that a central purpose of the Testnet ECS Ecosystem is to evaluate readiness for ECS governance and issuance. Accordingly, the Participant shall submit to, cooperate with, and fully comply with all audit, assessment, and compliance simulation activities established by the Foundation, including but not limited to:

(a) **Annual audits**, conducted to evaluate general operational readiness, issuance accuracy, governance discipline, and schema adherence;

(b) **Random audits**, initiated without prior notice to assess behavioral consistency, process integrity, and compliance reliability;

(c) **Risk-based audits**, targeted at issuance events or schemas deemed by the Foundation to present heightened governance, trust, or interpretive risk;

(d) **Issuance event logging requirements**, mandating the complete, accurate, and timely recording of issuance activity, revocation events, credential metadata, integrity signals, and any additional fields required by the Foundation within the Testnet ECS Trust Registry;

(e) **Human-review obligations**, applicable to any testnet issuance exercises involving credential types categorized provisionally as medium- or high-assurance, for which the Foundation may require manual or semi-automated review steps;

(f) **Key management expectations**, including provisional practices relating to DID usage, cryptographic key rotation, secure storage, and prompt mitigation upon detection of key compromise, all of which simulate governance-level operational security but shall not constitute definitive key management requirements for mainnet, which will be defined exclusively under the EGF.

4.4 **Technical Integration Responsibilities.**

The Participant agrees to adhere to all technical processes, simulation protocols, integration guidelines, provisional schema workflows, and issuance instructions established by the Foundation for testnet credential exercises. These may include: (a) use of designated SDKs or test APIs; (b) participation in schema-evaluation sessions; (c) submission of mock issuance data; (d) simulation of revocation procedures; (e) compliance with temporary or draft ECS updates; and (f) engagement with ecosystem interoperability tests.

4.5 **Data Integrity and Corrective Action.**

The Participant shall ensure that all data used or submitted during testnet participation is appropriate for testing purposes, does not replicate or include regulated personal data except in anonymized or synthetic form, and is not harmful, defamatory, unlawful, or misleading. In the event that the Participant issues incorrect or noncompliant testnet credentials, fails to follow provisional issuance guidelines, or produces aberrant behavior in schema-governance simulations, the Participant shall promptly undertake corrective actions, including revocation of inaccurate credentials, resubmission of corrected issuance events, remediation of process errors, and participation in any review, diagnostic, or educational processes requested by the Foundation.

4.6 **Testnet Participation as Evaluation Only.**

The Participant acknowledges that participation in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem serves solely as an evaluative mechanism enabling the Foundation and, after mainnet launch, the Verana Council to develop perspectives regarding the Participant’s potential suitability for Issuer Grantor or Issuer roles. The Foundation shall have sole discretion to determine the significance, weight, or interpretive effect of testnet performance metrics, and may disregard or reweight such metrics at any time based on broader ecosystem considerations.

4.7 **No Operational Dependence on Testnet.**

The Participant shall not rely on the Testnet ECS Ecosystem for any operational process, production workflow, customer deployment, or commercial functionality. The Participant further acknowledges that: (a) the testnet may undergo interruptions, resets, reconfigurations, data purges, or schema revisions at any time; (b) the testnet is not designed for long-term data retention; and (c) no testnet credential shall be used or represented as a production-grade credential.

4.8 **Foundation’s Discretion Over Testnet Mechanics.**

The Foundation may, at any time, alter the Testnet ECS Ecosystem’s structure, operational logic, schemas, audit procedures, or governance simulation parameters, including but not limited to: adding or removing ECSs; modifying provisional workflow rules; adjusting issuance guidelines; changing the Trust Registry data model; and terminating or restarting the testnet entirely. The Participant shall have no claim, remedy, compensation entitlement, or legal recourse arising out of any such change.

4.9 **Interpretation Favoring Provisionality.**

All provisions of this Section 4 shall be interpreted so as to reinforce the provisional, experimental, and non-binding character of testnet activities. Nothing herein shall be construed as establishing any permanent or transferable governance role, credential authority, schema control, or issuance capacity.

(i) the EGF, once adopted, shall automatically supersede this MoU **in its entirety** without need for additional action, consent, or acknowledgment from either Party; and

(ii) all non-binding provisions of this MoU shall expire at that time, with only those provisions expressly designated as binding continuing in force.

12.2 **Termination by Either Party Without Cause.**

Either Party may terminate this MoU, in whole and without cause, at any time and for any reason or for no reason, by delivering not less than **ten (10) business days’ prior written notice** to the other Party. Such termination shall be effective upon expiration of the notice period unless a later date is expressly designated in the terminating Party’s notice.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.**

The Foundation may terminate this MoU immediately, without prior notice and without liability of any kind, in the event that the Foundation determines, in its sole and absolute discretion, that the Participant has:

(a) breached any binding provision of this MoU, including without limitation Sections 6 (Intellectual Property Rights), 7 (Confidentiality), or 8 (Restrictions);

(b) engaged in conduct inconsistent with the governance intent, integrity, security, or provisional rules of the Testnet ECS Ecosystem;

(c) misrepresented its status, role, authority, or relationship to the Verana Foundation, the Verana Council, or the ECS Ecosystem;

(d) failed to cooperate in or obstructed any audit, assessment, compliance simulation, or corrective-action request;

(e) introduced harmful, misleading, malicious, or otherwise inappropriate data or credentials into the Testnet ECS Ecosystem;

(f) engaged in conduct reasonably deemed by the Foundation to pose regulatory, operational, cybersecurity, reputational, or governance risk; or

(g) otherwise demonstrated unfitness or unsuitability for potential future participation in ECS governance or issuance roles.

The Participant may terminate this MoU immediately if the Foundation commits a material breach of this MoU and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) business days after receiving written notice thereof.

12.4 **Effect of Termination; No Continuing Rights.**

Upon termination or expiration of this MoU:

(a) the Participant shall immediately cease all participation in the Testnet ECS Ecosystem, including but not limited to issuance simulations, schema-governance simulations, access to testnet Trust Registry components, and any governance exercises;

(b) any provisional role, authorization, or evaluation status granted to the Participant shall automatically and irrevocably cease, with no expectation of reactivation or continuation;

(c) the Participant shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in accordance with Section 7.6;

(d) the Participant shall have **no right** to assert that its prior participation conveys any benefit, entitlement, recognition, or favorable standing for mainnet consideration; and

(e) the Foundation shall have no obligation to provide transition assistance or continue any engagement with the Participant.

12.5 **Automatic Supersession by the Ecosystem Governance Framework (EGF).**

Upon adoption of the EGF by the Verana Council, this MoU—other than the binding provisions identified in Section 12.6—shall be automatically and fully superseded. The Participant acknowledges and agrees that:

(a) the EGF shall serve as the exclusive governing instrument for any mainnet ECS participation;

(b) no term of this MoU shall be construed to conflict with or restrict the authority of the Verana Council under the EGF; and

(c) any future participation by the Participant shall be subject to acceptance and compliance with the EGF.

12.6 **Survival of Binding Obligations.**

Notwithstanding any termination, expiration, or supersession of this MoU, the following Sections shall survive and continue in full force and effect in accordance with their respective terms: **Section 6 (Intellectual Property Rights); Section 7 (Confidentiality); Section 8 (Restrictions); Section 9 (Disclaimers); Section 10 (Governing Law); Section 11 (Dispute Resolution); and this Section 12 (Term, Termination, and Survival)**. These surviving obligations shall remain enforceable:

(a) irrespective of whether the Participant is ultimately selected for or admitted into any mainnet ECS role;

(b) irrespective of any decision by the Verana Council concerning ECS-related accreditation; and

(c) irrespective of whether the EGF supersedes this MoU.

12.7 **No Damages or Compensation.**

To the fullest extent permissible under applicable law, neither Party shall be liable to the other Party for any damages, losses, costs, liabilities, claims, or expenses arising solely from termination or expiration of this MoU, including without limitation: lost profits, reliance damages, opportunity costs, reputational harm, speculative losses, or costs associated with the cessation of testnet participation. The Participant acknowledges that testnet participation is voluntary and undertaken with full understanding of its provisional and revocable nature.

12.8 **Interpretation Favoring Limited Duration.**

All provisions of this Section 12 shall be interpreted in a manner consistent with the explicitly transitional, pre-mainnet, and time-limited character of this MoU. Any ambiguity shall be resolved to reinforce that the MoU is temporary, provisional, and subordinate to the Ecosystem Governance Framework.
