Chronology

From spec to network to governance body.

A chronology of the Verana Council and the public trust infrastructure it governs.

  1. 2023

    Initial work on the Verifiable Trust specification begins at Verana Labs.

  2. 2024

    Verifiable Trust v1–v3 drafts published. First testnet deployments. Trust over IP community contributes to specification leadership.

  3. 2025 Q2

    Verifiable Trust v4 and VPR spec published under CC-BY-SA 4.0.

  4. 2025 Q4

    Testnet live: Layer-1 chain (Cosmos SDK based), indexer, resolver, visualizer, faucet.

  5. 2026 Q1

    Pre-formation consortium begins signing Letters of Intent with prospective Founding Members. Founding Steward role formalized.

  6. 2026 Q2

    First IIW presentations of the Verifiable Trust Protocol. This site (veranacouncil.org) published.

  7. 2026 Q3 (target)

    Verana Council Association incorporation (Swiss Verein). LOIs ratified into Membership Agreements. Bylaws, Network GF, ECS-EGF, and Template EGF formally adopted.

  8. 2027 Q3 (target)

    Mainnet launch. Validator set restricted to Council Members.
